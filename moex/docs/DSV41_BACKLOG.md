# DeepSeek-V4.1 on this box: the backlog

The work not done yet, in one place: each item with what it buys (measured, or an estimate said as one), what it costs,
and where it stands. The measurements behind the numbers are in DSV41_PLAN.md §11-§13. Keep this list current: add an item
when it is deferred, move it to "Done" or "Dropped" with its number when it lands.

The goal (the user, 2026-10-18): the best quality/speed quant; the highest prefill and decode speeds this box allows,
with route bias, DSpark and every other lever on "without noticeable degradation in quality"; the theoretical speed to
match the measured one. "Noticeable" is measured as at most +0.02 KLD and -1 point of same-top against the exact pack
(about twice the engine's own run-to-run floor, KLD 0.010).

Kind: **exact** = the output is unchanged (or differs only by the engine's rounding floor); **lossy** = it changes the
output and must stay opt-in, inside the quality threshold.
